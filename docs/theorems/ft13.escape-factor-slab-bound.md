## 1. Statement

All quantities are dimensionless reals. The profile coordinate $x$ may be read as frequency;
the formal statement integrates over $\mathbb R$ with Lebesgue measure.

### 1.1 Symbols

| Symbol | Lean name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $\psi$ | `ψ` | line profile $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $0 \le \psi \le 1$; it need not attain 1 |
| $\tau_0$ | `τ0` | depth scale: the depth at $x$ is $\tau_0\psi(x) \le \tau_0$; it is the line-centre depth only when $\psi$ peaks at 1, which is not assumed |
| $x$ | `x` | profile coordinate over which the Bochner integral is taken |

### 1.2 Definitions used

The flat-slab self-absorption factor (`selfAbsorptionFactor`) is

$$
SA(\tau)=
\begin{cases}
1, & \tau=0,\\[2pt]
\dfrac{1-e^{-\tau}}{\tau}, & \tau \neq 0.
\end{cases}
$$

The profile equivalent width (`equivWidth`) is

$$
W(\psi,\tau)=\int_{\mathbb R}\bigl(1-e^{-\tau\psi(x)}\bigr)\,dx.
$$

### 1.3 Hypotheses

- **H1** (`hψ0`) — $0 \le \psi$ as a function order, i.e. pointwise $0 \le \psi(x)$.
- **H2** (`hψ1`) — $\psi(x) \le 1$ for every $x$.
- **H3** (`hint`) — $\psi$ is Lebesgue/Bochner integrable over $\mathbb R$ with respect to volume.
- **H4** (`hτ`) — $0 < \tau_0$.

### 1.4 Conclusion

Under H1–H4,

$$
SA(\tau_0)\,\tau_0\int_{\mathbb R}\psi(x)\,dx \le W(\psi,\tau_0).
$$

When $\int_{\mathbb R}\psi(x)\,dx > 0$, this says that the profile-integrated escape factor
$W(\psi,\tau_0)/(\tau_0\int_{\mathbb R}\psi(x)\,dx)$ is at least the flat-slab factor
$SA(\tau_0)$ [derivation, unchecked].

## 2. Physical meaning

In the homogeneous-slab model, the optical depth at profile coordinate $x$ is
$\tau_0\psi(x)$. The quantity $W(\psi,\tau_0)$ is the equivalent width; for a homogeneous slab
with a frequency-independent source function $S$, the integrated emergent intensity is $S\,W$,
and its optically thin value is $S\,\tau_0\int\psi$. The headline theorem says that the
frequency profile cannot make the integrated escape factor smaller than the flat-slab escape
factor at depth $\tau_0$ (the line-centre depth when $\psi$ peaks at 1): the wings, where
$\psi(x) < 1$, are less self-absorbed than the line centre.

If $\tau_0$ is identified with the physical line-centre depth, then dividing a profile-integrated
intensity by the flat-slab factor $SA(\tau_0)$ over-corrects in the model sense [derivation,
unchecked]. The member is a real inequality. Combined with the identity
$\frac{d}{d\tau}\log SA(\tau)=\frac{1}{e^\tau-1}-\frac1\tau$ for $\tau>0$
(`hasDerivAt_log_selfAbsorptionFactor`), it puts the slope of $\log SA$ in $(-1/2,0)$; the
resulting $1/2$-Lipschitz statement is `log_selfAbsorptionFactor_lipschitz`. Both landed after
this card and are not members of it.

- **Hypothesis sharpness / non-vacuity:** the rectangular profile

  $1/0 = 0$) and $\tau = -1$ gives $\approx 0.58 > 1/2$ [derivation, unchecked].

## 4. Proof idea

The headline proof rewrites the left-hand side using H4 and the definition of
`selfAbsorptionFactor`, so that it becomes the integral of
$(1-e^{-\tau_0})\psi(x)$. The pointwise helper `slab_chord_le` proves, from `convexOn_exp`, that
for $t \in [0,1]$,

$$
(1-e^{-\tau})t \le 1-e^{-\tau t}.
$$

Applying this with $t=\psi(x)$ and integrating uses `integral_mono`. Integrability of the two
integrands comes from `hint.const_mul` and `equivWidth_integrand_integrable`.

The member proof proves the lower bound from `Real.add_one_lt_exp` and
`one_div_lt_one_div_of_lt`. The upper bound clears denominators to the positivity of
$e^\tau(\tau-2)+\tau+2$, proved by the private helper `exp_mul_sub_two_add_pos` using
`strictMonoOn_of_hasDerivWithinAt_pos` and `hasDerivAt_exp_mul_sub_two_add`. The final algebraic
step uses `div_sub_div`, `div_lt_div_iff₀`, and `nlinarith`.

  factor, once the derivative identity in section 2 is separately established.
- **What it would change:** It would change rigor, not accuracy. It would allow the pipeline to
  state a proved relationship between flat-slab and profile-integrated self-absorption, and a
  proved inequality that is the key step toward, but is not, a slope bound for $\log SA$, without
  claiming that any particular measurement is made more accurate.
- **How it would be validated:** Validate by evaluating `doppler_cog_escape_factor(τ0)` against
