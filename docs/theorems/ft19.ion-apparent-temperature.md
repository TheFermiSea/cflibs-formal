## 1. Statement

All quantities are dimensionless reals (the repo's bare-`ℝ` core, `CONTEXT.md` design decision
1). Level energies of a stage are measured from that stage's own ground level
(`docs/conventions.md` §2).

### 1.1 Symbols

| Symbol | Lean | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| $\zeta,\iota,\kappa$ | `ζ`,`ι`,`κ` | finite nonempty index types: zones, neutral levels, ion levels |
| $k_B,m_e,h$ | `kB`,`me`,`h` | Boltzmann constant, electron mass, Planck constant |
| $\chi$ | `chi` | ionization energy of the neutral |
| $n_e$ | `ne` | electron density, one value shared by all zones |
| $F$ | `Fcal` | calibration constant, shared by all zones and both stages |
| $T_z$ | `T z` | temperature of zone $z$ |
| $N^{\rm I}_z,N^{\rm II}_z$ | `NI` `z`,`NII` `z` | neutral, ion density of zone $z$ |
| $g_k,E_k,A_k$ | `gZ` `k`,`EZ` `k`,`AI` `k` | neutral level degeneracy, energy, transition probability |
| $g'_k,E'_k,A'_k$ | `gZ1` `k`,`EZ1` `k`,`AII` `k` | the same for the ion stage |
| $i,j,i',j'$ | `i`,`j`,`i'`,`j'` | the two neutral levels, then the two ion levels, of the pairs read |

### 1.2 Definitions used

$$
U_{\rm I}(T)=\sum_{k\in\iota} g_k e^{-E_k/(k_BT)},\quad U_{\rm II}(T)=\sum_{k\in\kappa} g'_k e^{-E'_k/(k_BT)},\quad
\theta(T)=\frac{2\pi m_e k_B T}{h^2}\ (\texttt{thermalBracket})
$$

$$
S(T)=2\,\frac{U_{\rm II}(T)}{U_{\rm I}(T)}\,\theta(T)^{3/2}\,e^{-\chi/(k_BT)}\qquad(\texttt{sahaFactor})
$$

$$
I^{\rm I}_k=\sum_{z\in\zeta}\frac{F A_k N^{\rm I}_z g_k e^{-E_k/(k_BT_z)}}{U_{\rm I}(T_z)},\qquad
I^{\rm II}_k=\sum_{z\in\zeta}\frac{F A'_k N^{\rm II}_z g'_k e^{-E'_k/(k_BT_z)}}{U_{\rm II}(T_z)}
\qquad(\texttt{mixedLineIntensity})
$$

$$
y_k=\ln\frac{I^{\rm I}_k}{g_kA_k},\ y'_k=\ln\frac{I^{\rm II}_k}{g'_kA'_k},\qquad
\beta_{\rm app}(E_1,y_1,E_2,y_2)=\frac{y_1-y_2}{E_2-E_1}\qquad(\texttt{apparentBeta})
$$

### 1.3 Hypotheses

- **H1** (`hkB`,`hme`,`hh`,`hchi`,`hne`,`hFcal`) — $k_B,m_e,h,n_e,F>0$ and $\chi\ge 0$.
- **H2** (`hT`,`hNI`) — $T_z>0$ and $N^{\rm I}_z>0$ for every zone.
- **H3** (`hgZ`,`hgZ1`,`hAI`,`hAII`) — $g_k,A_k>0$ on $\iota$; $g'_k,A'_k>0$ on $\kappa$.
- **H4** (`hNII`) — Saha balance at one shared $n_e$: $N^{\rm II}_z=N^{\rm I}_z\,S(T_z)/n_e$ for every zone.
- **H5** (`hij`,`hij'`) — a neutral pair `i j` with $E_i \lt E_j$; an ion pair `i' j'` with $E'_{i'} \lt E'_{j'}$.
- **H6** (`hanchor`) — $E_j\le E'_{i'}$: the whole ion pair lies above the whole neutral pair.

### 1.4 Conclusion

$$
\beta^{\rm II}:=\beta_{\rm app}(E'_{i'},y'_{i'},E'_{j'},y'_{j'})\ \le\ \beta_{\rm app}(E_i,y_i,E_j,y_j)=:\beta^{\rm I}.
$$

### 1.5 Lean correspondence

| Binder | Label | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `hchi` | H1 | `0 ≤ chi`; every other H1 binder is a strict `<` |
| `hNII` | H4 | parses as $(N^{\rm I}_z\,S(T_z))/n_e$, a single Lean `/` |
| `hij`,`hij'` | H5 | strict `<`; these are what keep `apparentBeta`'s denominator $E_2-E_1$ away from Lean's total `a / 0 = 0` |
| `hanchor` | H6 | non-strict `≤` |

The `(3 / 2 : ℝ)` exponent in `sahaFactor`/`ionReweight` is `Real.rpow`, not `ℕ` division (which
would silently give exponent `1`); confirmed against the built environment
[`scratch: evidence/ft19.ion-apparent-temperature/check2.lean`](evidence/ft19.ion-apparent-temperature/check2.lean). No
hidden `a / 0 = 0`: H3 plus `Fintype`/`Nonempty` make every partition function strictly
positive, so the reciprocals inside `sahaFactor` and `zoneWeight` never divide by zero.

### 1.6 Members: `ionReweight` and `ionReweight_strictMonoOn`

$$
\rho(T)=\frac{2\,\theta(T)^{3/2}\,e^{-\chi/(k_BT)}}{n_e}
$$

`ionReweight` is a `def`, not a theorem: the thermal part of `sahaFactor` divided by `n_e`, with
the partition-function ratio $U_{\rm II}/U_{\rm I}$ removed; binders `kB`,`me`,`h`,`chi`,`ne`,`T`
are kB, me, h, chi, ne as above and a scalar temperature T (the headline applies it at `T z`),
unconstrained (no hypotheses on a `def`). `ionReweight_strictMonoOn`
adds hypotheses `hkB`,`hme`,`hh`,`hchi`,`hne` (H1 without hFcal) and concludes `ionReweight kB me h chi
ne` is `StrictMonoOn` on `Set.Ioi 0`. Unlike `sahaFactor_strictMonoOn_temp`, no level-truncation
hypothesis is needed, because $U_{\rm II}/U_{\rm I}$ has already cancelled out of $\rho$.

### 1.7 Member: `tiltMean_reweight_le`

Symbols new to this member: `w`,`b`,`ρ` (functions $\zeta\to\mathbb R$: a zone weight, a zone
inverse temperature, a reweight), `a` (the anchor energy), `hw`,`hρ`,`hanti` (their hypotheses).

- **H1'** (`hw`) — $w_z>0$. **H2'** (`hρ`) — $\rho_z>0$. **H3'** (`hanti`) — $\rho$ is antivariant
  with $b$: $b_i \lt b_j\Rightarrow\rho_j\le\rho_i$.
- Conclusion, for any `a`: $\mathrm{tiltMean}(w\cdot\rho, b, a)\le\mathrm{tiltMean}(w,b,a)$, where
  $\mathrm{tiltMean}(w,b,a)=\sum_z \dfrac{w_z e^{-ab_z}}{\sum_{z'}w_{z'}e^{-ab_{z'}}}\,b_z$.

Pure math (weighted Chebyshev sum inequality); no physical hypothesis at all.

### 1.8 Member: `ion_zoneWeight_eq`

Hypotheses `hne`,`hgZ`,`hgZ1`,`hNII` (a subset of H1/H3/H4 above). Conclusion: under H4,
`zoneWeight kB Fcal T NII gZ1 EZ1 = fun z => zoneWeight kB Fcal T NI gZ EZ z * ionReweight kB me h
chi ne (T z)` — the ion zone weight equals the neutral zone weight times `ionReweight`: the ion
partition function cancels and the neutral one becomes the neutral zone weight's, via `field_simp`.
This is the bridge the headline theorem composes with `tiltMean_reweight_le`.

## 2. Physical meaning

**Setting.** One element observed through a line of sight crossing several plasma regions
(zones — the same formalism covers time slices of a gate, or accumulated shots; H4's single $n_e$
is a strong assumption for time slices, over which $n_e$ decays), each in LTE at
its own temperature, all sharing one electron density, with optically thin emission summing the
zone contributions. Two classical two-line Boltzmann temperatures are read off the spectrum: one
from a neutral pair, one from an ion pair.

**Mechanism.** In each zone, Saha balance fixes the ion-to-neutral ratio at $S(T_z)/n_e$. After
the partition functions cancel (`ion_zoneWeight_eq`), the ion zone weights are the neutral ones
times $\rho(T_z)$, increasing with temperature (`ionReweight_strictMonoOn`), so ion emission comes
preferentially from hotter zones — and because the ion pair lies higher in energy (H6), whose
Boltzmann factors also favour hot zones (the tilted mean is antitone in its anchor energy). The
ion Boltzmann plot therefore averages the zone inverse
temperatures with weight tilted toward hot zones (`tiltMean_reweight_le`), giving a flatter slope:
$\beta^{\rm II}\le\beta^{\rm I}$.

**Homogeneous case.** If every zone shares one temperature, both apparent inverse temperatures
equal $1/(k_BT)$ [proved: CflibsFormal.apparentBeta_eq_homogeneous], applied to the two
log-mixture ordinates (`mixed_boltzmann_ordinate`, `ion_zoneWeight_eq`), and the inequality is an
equality. Within the model: a measured $T^{\rm II}_{\rm app} \gt T^{\rm I}_{\rm app}$ is
consistent with an inhomogeneous line of sight
[scratch: evidence/ft19.ion-apparent-temperature/ft19_temperature.lean]; a measured
$T^{\rm II}_{\rm app} \lt T^{\rm I}_{\rm app}$ beyond noise
contradicts at least one hypothesis: per-zone LTE in a finite mixture, ideal Saha balance (no IPD)
at one shared $n_e$, optical thinness, one calibration $F$ for both stages, exact atomic data
($g$, $A$, $E$, $\chi$), a two-line (not least-squares) reading, or the anchor H6.

**Illustration, numerics only** [`numerics: evidence/ft19_illustration.py`] — two zones at 8000 K
and 14000 K, textbook (not repo-sourced) $n_e,\chi$: reweights $\rho\approx0.36$ (cool),
$\approx114$ (hot); an anchored neutral pair reads $T^{\rm I}_{\rm app}\approx12082$ K against an
ion pair's $\approx13998$ K. Not a real line list — the theorem gives the sign, not the gap.

## 3. Scope and validity

The headline theorem publishes **REDUCED** (own REDUCED; its statement uses `sahaFactor` and
`mixedLineIntensity`'s `lineIntensity`, both REDUCED model rows; scope-check lists no `via`
because the relation tag is already REDUCED); so does
`ion_zoneWeight_eq`. `ionReweight_strictMonoOn` and `tiltMean_reweight_le` publish **PURE-MATH**
(exempt from the model axis). `ionReweight` itself is a `def` carrying its own model tag
REDUCED (`docs/scope-tags.tsv`).

**Idealizations encoded:**
1. A finite mixture of zones, each in LTE (Boltzmann populations) at its own $T_z$ — no non-LTE, no continuous state distribution.
2. Optically thin emission — zone intensities add; no self-absorption, no re-absorption by cooler outer layers.
3. `lineIntensity`'s $\lambda$-free photon-rate line form.
4. One $F$ shared by every zone and both stages.
5. Ideal Saha balance at one $n_e$ for all zones: no ionization-potential depression (D18) and the literal finite level sum (D17).
6. Two-line pairs, not the $n$-line least-squares slope.
7. The anchor H6, which the module's own docstring calls "restrictive and may fail for the line pairs used in practice."
8. Noiseless intensities and exact atomic data.

**What it does not claim:**
- a strict gap for inhomogeneous zones; the temperature form (landed statement is in inverse
  temperature);
- the size of the gap between the two apparent temperatures — sign only;
- anything about the $n$-line OLS slopes the pipeline actually fits;
- anything when $n_e$ varies between zones — the audit notes $\rho$ can fail to be antivariant if $n_{e,z}$ rises with $T_z$ fast enough (`frontier.json` FT-19 `hypotheses_rationale`);
- composition accuracy, or the size of the pooled Saha–Boltzmann slope bias;
- that the Aguilera–Aragón measurement is *explained*: their plasma has spatially resolved $n_e$ and their plots are multi-line, so the theorem is consistent with, and does not prove, that measurement.

**Hypothesis sharpness / non-vacuity.** The hypotheses are jointly satisfiable on genuinely
different zones: `ft19_witness.lean` instantiates the theorem on `T = ![1, 2]` with distinct
neutral and ion level lists [`scratch: evidence/ft19_witness.lean`]. H6 is not necessary, only
sufficient — the not-landed FT-19d variant (§6) replaces it with the weaker $E_j\le E'_{i'}+\chi$
[scratch: evidence/ft19.ion-apparent-temperature/ft19d.lean].

## 4. Proof idea

The assembly chains four inequalities (`logMixture_chain`, private,
`IonApparentTemperature.lean`), writing $w_z=F N^{\rm I}_z/U_{\rm I}(T_z)$ and $b_z=1/(k_BT_z)$:

$$
\beta^{\rm II}\ \le\ \langle b\rangle^{w\rho}_{E'_{i'}}\ \le\ \langle b\rangle^{w}_{E'_{i'}}\ \le\ \langle b\rangle^{w}_{E_j}\ \le\ \beta^{\rm I}.
$$

1. **Ordinates are log-mixtures.** `mixed_boltzmann_ordinate` gives $y_k=\ln\sum_zw_ze^{-E_kb_z}$; `ion_zoneWeight_eq` turns the ion weights into $w_z\rho(T_z)$ (private `ion_ordinate` packages both).
2. **First step**, `pairSlope_le_tiltMean` — the two-point slope is at most the tilted mean at the lower line (Jensen for `exp`, `logMixture_tangent_le`).
3. **Second step**, `tiltMean_reweight_le` — weighted Chebyshev; the antivariance of $\rho$ in $b$ is the private `ionReweight_anti`, from `ionReweight_strictMonoOn` (`thermalBracket_strictMono` plus `Real.rpow_lt_rpow`).
4. **Third step**, `tiltMean_le_pairSlope` then `pairSlope_le_tiltMean` on the neutral mixture's
   auxiliary pair $(E_j, E'_{i'})$ (equality if $E_j = E'_{i'}$) — the tilted mean is antitone in
   its anchor. **Fourth step**, `tiltMean_le_pairSlope` on $(E_i, E_j)$.

## 5. Role in the composition-extraction pipeline

- **Current state.** No pipeline code or doc references this theorem, its module, or its lemma
  names (`rg` for `InhomogeneityBias|IonApparentTemperature|tiltMean|apparentBeta|ionReweight`
  over `cflibs/` and `docs/` at `main@b142ae25`: zero hits, against a `cflibs-formal` positive
  control that matched dozens of generic mentions in the same search — so the null result is the
  search finding nothing, not failing to run; the Lean identifier `sahaBoltzmann_shift_eq_log_saha`
  also returns hits in the same search, confirming the pattern form works). Four sites
  (`pipeline[]` above) assume stage-equal apparent temperatures, which FT-19 bounds one-sidedly
  ($\beta^{\rm II}\le\beta^{\rm I}$, equality in the homogeneous case; a strict gap for
  inhomogeneous zones is not proved — open item 3, and is only illustrated numerically in §2):
  per-element temperature averaging, the pooled Saha–Boltzmann graph's one shared slope, the
  default intercept route's shared-plane fit, and the default $n_e$ route's shared-slope offset.
- **Proposed use (not implemented).** A stage-ordering diagnostic in `QualityAssessor`: report
  $\beta^{\rm I}$ and $\beta^{\rm II}$ separately, with a one-sided statistic
  $D=\beta^{\rm I}-\beta^{\rm II}$ and its propagated uncertainty. Under this theorem's model
  $D\ge0$; a measured $D$ far below zero flags that the shared-slope assumptions are unsupported
  for that spectrum, rather than accepting the pooled fit silently. Restricted to well-separated
  two-line pairs satisfying H6 until §6's $n$-line item is closed.
- **What it would change.** The reliability flags and the choice of estimator route — never the
  composition arithmetic itself, and never pitched as a measurement-accuracy improvement
  (`AGENTS.md`: rigor, not accuracy).
- **How it would be validated.** Against `TwoZoneSpectrumModel`'s synthetic spectra
  (`cflibs/radiation/two_zone.py`), whose default already shares $n_e$ (matches H4) but applies
  Debye–Hückel IPD by default (which `ionReweight` omits, so the validation must disable IPD or
  treat it as a deviation), in the thin-emission limit (both zones optically thin) its
  re-absorption model reduces to: expect $D\ge0$ whenever
  $T_{\rm core}\ne T_{\rm periph}$ on an anchored pair, $D\approx0$ when the zones share one
  temperature. **Falsification arm:** override the model's per-zone $n_e$ so it rises steeply with
  $T$ (breaking H4) and check whether $D<0$ can then occur; if it never does, the diagnostic
  cannot distinguish that violation.
