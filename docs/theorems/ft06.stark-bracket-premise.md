## 2. Physical meaning

The theorem formalizes the Stark-side error bar for an electron-density estimate from a line
width. The estimate `CflibsFormal.starkDensity` inverts the linear electron-impact law
`CflibsFormal.starkFWHM`. If the tabulated width parameter is only known to within a
multiplicative grade `ρw`, and the measured width may be larger than the pure Stark width by at
most the opacity budget `kOpac`, then the estimate can be too large or too small by those
multiplicative factors: the measured width is at least the true Stark width and at most `kOpac`
times it. The lower end divides the estimate by `kOpac·ρw`, and the upper end multiplies it by
`ρw`. The estimate can therefore overshoot the true density by up to a factor `kOpac·ρw` and
undershoot it by up to a factor `ρw`. The assumptions are inputs to the bracket, not measurements
derived by the theorem [derivation, unchecked]. The Lean statement assumes only
$0 < \texttt{kOpac}$, and that hypothesis is not load-bearing: for $\texttt{neTrue} > 0$, H7 and
H8 together force $1 \le \texttt{kOpac}$, and at $\texttt{neTrue} = 0$ both ends hold for any
$\texttt{kOpac}$. A budget below 1 makes the premises unsatisfiable; it does not give a narrower
bracket.

## 3. Scope and validity

The generated scope-tags line will be rendered in the Facts block; this section describes the
idealizations without retyping tags.

- **Idealizations encoded:** the linear electron-impact width law is used, and ion broadening is
  not modelled [derivation, unchecked]. Opacity is represented by a single positive multiplicative
  budget [derivation, unchecked]. The tabulated parameter grade is a closed multiplicative
  interval, and `ρw` and `kOpac` are assumed inputs rather than inferred quantities
  [derivation, unchecked]. All quantities are dimensionless reals. Per the docstring, `w`,
  `wTrue` and `widthMeas` share a wavelength unit, `nRef` and `neTrue` share a density unit, `ρw`
  and `kOpac` are pure numbers, and the conclusion is homogeneous in each. No `Dimensions.lean`
  theorem checks this for `starkFWHM` or `starkDensity`.
- **What it does NOT claim:** it does not prove that the measured width is actually an
  opacity-broadened Stark width; it assumes H7 and H8 [derivation, unchecked]. It takes `kOpac`
  as given and does not derive its value from the spectrum; for `neTrue > 0` the premises merely
  force `kOpac ≥ 1`. It does not derive `ρw` from data [derivation, unchecked]. It does not prove
  the two-diagnostic C8 certificate, the refusal lemma, or that McWhirter's LTE criterion passes;
  those require additional premises and are not in this statement [derivation, unchecked].
  `starkSaha_certificate_sound`, `starkSaha_refusal_sound` and `saha_ipd_bracket` are proved only
  in audit evidence (`docs/research/audit-2026-09-24/evidence/frontier-proposer/Proofs.lean`,
  `frontier-verifier/Verify.lean`), not landed. It does not claim improved measurement accuracy or
  calibration-free operation; it formalizes bounds under stated model and inputs
  [derivation, unchecked]. It does not model ion Stark broadening or other broadening mechanisms
  [derivation, unchecked]. `w` is fixed at its reference value; any temperature dependence of the
  width parameter must be absorbed into `ρw`. `widthMeas` is whatever width is fed to
  `starkDensity`, so any instrumental or Doppler width not removed beforehand must also fit
  inside `kOpac` for H8 to hold. Width laws that are not linear in `n_e` are outside `starkFWHM`.
- **Hypothesis sharpness / non-vacuity:** a non-degenerate witness is
  `(w, ρw, kOpac, wTrue, nRef, neTrue, widthMeas) = (1, 2, 3/2, 1, 1, 1, 3)`, for which the
  Stark estimate is `3/2` and the bracket is `[1/2, 3]`, containing `neTrue = 1`
  [scratch: evidence/ft06.stark-bracket-premise/ft06_probe.lean]. `hnRef` is load-bearing: at
  `nRef = -1` (with `hne` satisfied) the lower end fails
  [scratch: evidence/ft06.stark-bracket-premise/ft06_probe.lean]. `hne` is load-bearing even once
  `hnRef` holds: at `neTrue = -1` the upper end fails
  [scratch: evidence/ft06.stark-bracket-premise/FT06-guards-evidence.lean]. `hρ` excludes the
  degenerate `ρw = 0` case (Lean's `x / 0 = 0` convention), which breaks the upper end
  [scratch: evidence/ft06.stark-bracket-premise/FT06-rho0-guard-evidence.lean]. `hk` is not
  load-bearing: `stark_bracket_rho_noK` proves the same conclusion from `hw`, `hρ`, `hnRef`,
  `hne`, `hwT`, `hopac` and `hbudget` alone
  [scratch: evidence/ft06.stark-bracket-premise/ft06_noK.lean].
- **Related declarations:** at `ρw = 1` this is `starkDensity_brackets_true` restricted to
  `neTrue ≥ 0` [proved: CflibsFormal.starkDensity_brackets_true].

## 4. Proof idea

The proof unfolds `CflibsFormal.starkFWHM` and `CflibsFormal.starkDensity`, multiplies H7 and H8
by `0 < nRef` to obtain the two product bounds, and then clears denominators using positivity of
`w`, `ρw`, `kOpac`, and `nRef`. The main algebraic facts are `div_le_iff₀`, `le_div_iff₀`,
`div_mul_cancel₀`, `div_mul_eq_mul_div`, `mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_right`, `div_pos`,
`lt_of_lt_of_le`, and `mul_assoc`; `linarith`, `positivity`, and `nlinarith` close the linear
and quadratic inequalities [derivation, unchecked].

## 5. Role in the composition-extraction pipeline

- **Current state:** landed as a standalone theorem in `CflibsFormal/StarkOpacityGuard.lean`. It
  is not wired into CF-LIBS-improved; in particular it is not part of a runtime C8 gate in
  `cflibs/evolution/certificate_gate.py`, for example through `hard_certificate_gate`
  [derivation, unchecked].
- **Proposed use:** supply the Stark bracket endpoints for a future C8 Stark-Saha bracket: lower
  endpoint `starkDensity w nRef widthMeas / (kOpac * ρw)`, upper endpoint
  `starkDensity w nRef widthMeas * ρw`. A C8 soundness statement would then combine these with a
  Saha bracket, using only lower endpoints for McWhirter soundness and treating disagreement as
  refusal [derivation, unchecked]. For `ρw = 1`, a slab-Lorentzian model derives the budget from
  `τ` [proved: CflibsFormal.starkOpacity_certificate_sound_of_tau]. Extending that to discharge
  H8 here is not done.
- **What it would change:** it would add a theorem-named multiplicative error bound for the Stark
  diagnostic and a named premise for a sound refusal. It would not improve measurement accuracy;
  it would make the assumptions and bounds explicit and checkable [derivation, unchecked].
- **How it would be validated:** on synthetic spectra with known `neTrue` and `wTrue`, broadened
  by a factor `k ≤ kOpac` and with the tabulated `w` inside the `ρw` grade, the bracket the
  pipeline computes must contain `neTrue`. Falsification arm: fixtures with `k > kOpac` or
  `wTrue` outside the grade must be able to produce a bracket that excludes `neTrue` (the check
  can fail). A fixture that satisfies the premises and yields a bracket excluding `neTrue` would
  show that the pipeline computes something other than `starkDensity`/`starkFWHM`.
