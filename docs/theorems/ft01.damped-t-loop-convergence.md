## 2. Physical meaning

The companion's outer loop alternates a Boltzmann/Saha-Boltzmann fit that yields a temperature
with an electron-density update, relaxing each step by one half: the new temperature is the
equal-weight (1/2-1/2) average of the previous temperature and the freshly fitted one
(`IterativeCFLIBSSolver._run_python_iteration`; the JAX path mirrors it). That is
exactly `dampedMap` at `lam = 1/2`, evaluated at the previous temperature, where the undamped
update `g` (1.6's function, not 1.1's number) is the pipeline's slope-derived temperature at the
current `n_e`.

**`dampedMap_contracts`.** $g'$ is the sensitivity of the undamped update to its own input. For
$\lambda=1/2$ the window is $g' \in (-3,1)$: damping by one half tames oscillating (negative)
feedback down to a slope of $-3$, but does nothing for self-reinforcing feedback at $g'\ge 1$ —
that case is the unlanded `dampedMap_repelling`, an informal remark here, not a theorem.

**`tDamped_mobius_converges`.** Its model is that the undamped update is affine in inverse
temperature, $u' = gu+c$ with $u=1/T$ ($k_B$ absorbed); in $T$ that is the Möbius map $\varphi$.
The gain range $0 \lt g \lt 1$ is the physically relevant one `[derivation, unchecked]`: every
positive start converges to $T^\star=(1-g)/c$ (the proof runs through a monotone orbit, but the
exported statement asserts convergence only). At $g=-1$ the $T$-damped iterates go negative
(docstring remark) `[derivation, unchecked]`, which is why `hg0` cannot be dropped (a difference
from the $u$-damped idealization, whose window is the wider $1-2/\lambda \lt g \lt 1$ and which
does tolerate $g<0$
`[derivation, unchecked]`; the $u$-damped window itself is `dampedAffine_tendsto_iff`, landed in
`JointConvergence` outside this card).

**`exists_weights_iff`.** The coefficients are Lipschitz bounds of the joint update on the box,
$|\Delta T'| \le a|\Delta T| + b|\Delta n_e|$ and $|\Delta n_e'| \le c|\Delta T| + d|\Delta n_e|$
(for a differentiable update, bounds on $|\partial T'/\partial T|$, $|\partial T'/\partial n_e|$,
$|\partial n_e'/\partial T|$, $|\partial n_e'/\partial n_e|$), not signed derivatives. The row-sum
gate $\max(a+b,c+d)<1$ is that of the abstract spine `jointOuterContraction_box`
(`SahaEquilibrium.lean`); `jointConvergence` instantiates it with $a=d=0$, and `outerLoop_contracts`
uses the product gate $L_1 \cdot L_2 < 1$ instead. Rescaling $n_e$ by $s$ sends $b\mapsto b/s$,
$c\mapsto sc$, so $a$, $d$, $bc$ are unit-invariant, and the theorem says a rescaling that passes
the row-sum gate exists iff the unit-invariant condition holds.

  $b=100$, $c=10^{-3}$ has $a+b>1$ (unweighted gate fails) but $bc=0.1<\tfrac14=(1-a)(1-d)$, and a
  witness scale $r=w_n/w_T\approx0.0035$ makes the weighted gate hold at $0.85$
  [numerics: evidence/ft01.damped-t-loop-convergence/numerics.py].

## 4. Proof idea

- **`dampedMap_lipschitz`.** $H'=1-\lambda+\lambda g' \in [1-\lambda+\lambda m,\,1-\lambda+\lambda M]
  \subset(-1,1)$ given H5-H6; `Convex.norm_image_sub_le_of_norm_hasDerivWithin_le` turns that
  derivative bound into a $q$-Lipschitz bound on the convex set `Set.Icc a b`.
- **`dampedMap_contracts`.** Restricts $H$ to the closed, hence complete, interval $[a,b]$
  (`hmaps` makes the restriction well-typed) and applies `ContractingWith.exists_fixedPoint'` (via
  the Lipschitz bound above) from two starting points; the two fixed points coincide because
  $q<1$.
- **`tDamped_mobius_converges`.** A private monotone-orbit lemma, `orbit_tendsto_of_monotone`, is
  instantiated with private helpers `tDampedMobius_{Ts_pos,fix,up,down,mono,cont,uniq}`: $H$ is
  increasing on $(0,\infty)$ (uses `hl1`), $H(T)\ge T$ below $T^\star$ and $\le T$ above it, so the
  orbit is monotone and bounded; its limit is a fixed point by continuity
  (`isFixedPt_of_tendsto_iterate`) and equals $T^\star$ by uniqueness. Deliberately not a
  contraction proof: $H'(T)\to 1-\lambda+\lambda/g>1$ as $T\to0^+$.
- **`exists_weights_iff`.** ($\Rightarrow$) `nlinarith` on $(br)(c/r)=bc$ for $r=w_n/w_T$.
  ($\Leftarrow$) a private constructive lemma `weights_of_gate` takes
  $r=\tfrac12\bigl(c/(1-d)+(1-a)/b\bigr)$ when $b>0$, and $r=c/(1-d)+1$ when $b=0$.
- All four theorems are in `JointConvergence.lean`'s `DampedLoopCertificate` section, whose section
  docstring states the FT-01/C11 framing directly; `dampedMap`'s own docstring names the two
  companion call sites this family is written against.

## 5. Role in the composition-extraction pipeline

**Current state.** The companion damps both `T` and `n_e` by one half in
`IterativeCFLIBSSolver._run_python_iteration` (and its JAX mirror,
`_run_lax_while_loop.body_fun`); the stop rule there requires both step sizes to be under fixed
tolerances and the Boltzmann fit to be non-degenerate (the Python path also requires a
non-degenerate composition); nothing in it bounds the distance to the fixed point. Defaults are a
20-iteration cap, a 100 K temperature tolerance and a 10% density tolerance
(`IterativeCFLIBSSolver.__init__`). An opt-in Anderson accelerator
(`IterativeCFLIBSSolver._solve_python`) reaches the map's fixed point (shared by the damped and
undamped maps, since $\lambda>0$) in a handful of steps, where the plain damped loop stops early,
by algebraically inverting the 1/2 damping, but ships off by default because that fixed point is
documented as biased hot. No C11 object exists on either side: `Certificates.lean` has no C11
`def` or soundness theorem, and `certificate_gate.py`'s `LEAN_CERTIFICATE_DEFS` has no C11 entry —
matching decision D16's "neither ID ever shipped."

**Proposed use.** Two stages, neither implemented:

1. *Diagnostics, no Lean dependency, no behaviour change.* Record per iteration the undamped
   update already computed, a secant estimate of the local gain, and the exit reason. The secant
   estimate is heuristic (it mixes in the `n_e` response); its purpose is only to show where a run
   sits relative to the proved window, not to certify anything.
2. *A `C11` predicate*, once the missing pieces land (the physics binding and the Gauss-Seidel
   gate; `residual_stop` is landed): a closed-form gain per median piece, the window check at $\lambda=1/2$, and a
   certified distance bound in place of the bare step-size test; abstention when the gain leaves
   $(0,1)$.

**What it would change.** A `converged` flag would come to mean "certified close to the map's
fixed point" instead of "the last step was small" — a change in what the stop condition
*guarantees*, not in the plasma temperature or composition the pipeline reports. Per this repo's
cardinal rule, none of this is pitched as improving measurement accuracy: the map's fixed point is
not claimed to be the physically correct temperature (Section 3), and the damping constant would
not change.

**How it would be validated (falsifiable).** On synthetic maps affine in $1/T$ with a known
$T^\star$ (`tDamped_mobius_converges`'s own setting), the reported distance bound must contain the
true distance on every run in $0<g<1$. **Falsification arm:** with the gain check disabled, a
synthetic $g \in [1, 1.1]$ run must yield a "certified" verdict that the run then contradicts (no
positive fixed point exists, since $(1-g)/c \le 0$ `[derivation, unchecked]`; `dampedMap_repelling`
is not landed), so the test must fail; with the check enabled the certifier must abstain. On real
spectra, the fixed point the diagnostic converges toward would be compared against the
Anderson-accelerated fixed point as an internal consistency check, not as a ground truth.
